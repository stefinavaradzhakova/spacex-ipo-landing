What is an IPO?

An IPO is when a private company sells shares to the public for the first time. It can create hype, volatility, and new market opportunities — but also risk.

Private
Closed circle

Shares are held by founders, employees, and a small circle of investors. No public market, no daily price.

Public
Open to anyone

Shares trade on an exchange like NYSE or NASDAQ. Anyone can buy or sell. Full disclosure, daily price discovery.

From Tesla IPO to today

Tesla went public in 2010 at $17 per share. Since then, the stock has grown dramatically, reaching over $400 per share at various points after stock-split adjustments and delivering gains of more than 2,000% from its IPO price.

Back then, it was a risky electric-car company. Today, it is one of the world's most watched stocks.
